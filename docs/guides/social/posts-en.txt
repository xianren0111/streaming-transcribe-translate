Preparing a base version of a Windows live-subtitle tool.

Local speech recognition, your OpenAI-compatible translation API, multilingual reading, one review/send language, floating subtitles, OBS and reviewed SRT/CSV export.

Supports 哔哩哔哩, YouTube and 猫耳FM, one platform at a time. Manual review is the default; platform login is required to send chat.

This is a Python project requiring dependencies and local models, not an installation-free EXE. APIs may charge fees or impose limits. Records are off by default. Review translations and keep keys/cookies private.

All images use isolated demo data. Chinese, Japanese and English guides are ready. Public name and project link will be added later.
